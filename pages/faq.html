Common Inquiries

What is TheNeoGrid's deployment time? +

Our standard deployment takes 48 hours for node setup.

What exactly is a "Node" in your pricing tiers? +

At TheNeoGrid, a "Node" represents a specific level of digital infrastructure. A Starter Node is a foundational web presence (up to 5 pages), while a Core Node acts as a centralized hub for e-commerce and API integrations. We use this terminology because we view every project as a vital connection point within your business’s digital grid.

Do you provide hosting for the websites you develop? +

Yes. Our Enterprise Grid package includes dedicated cloud hosting. For Starter and Advanced tiers, we handle the full deployment to platforms like GitHub or Netlify and assist with SSL configuration to ensure your site is secure and live from day one.

Can you help me move an existing site to your infrastructure? +

Absolutely. We specialize in system redesigns. Our process involves auditing your current user interface and database structure before migrating your data into a more scalable, modern framework built with HTML, CSS, and secure backend protocols.

How do I get a quote for the Premium and Enterprise Grid tier? +

Since both solutions often involve AI automation, full-stack complexity, and unlimited resource allocation, pricing is tailored to your specific goals. You can reach out via our Contact page to schedule a technical audit with our team, where we will assess your requirements and provide a custom quote.
